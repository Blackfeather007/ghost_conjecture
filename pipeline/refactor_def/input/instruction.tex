$\bullet$ Give lemma to show that Newton polygon of the product of two power series is the Minkowski sum of the Newton polygons of the two power series.

\begin{proposition}
    Let $F(t) = \sum_{n \geq 0} f_n t^n$ and $G(t) = \sum_{n \geq 0} g_n t^n$ be two power series in $\QQ_p\llbracket t\rrbracket$. Then the Newton polygon of $F(t)G(t)$ is the Minkowski sum of the Newton polygons of $F(t)$ and $G(t)$.
\end{proposition}
